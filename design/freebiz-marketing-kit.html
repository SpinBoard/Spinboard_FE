Objection handling

"What's the catch?" — Brands pay to be on the board. We spend part of it on the prizes. You watch, you might catch one.

"Is the cash real?" — It goes to your wallet and we pay out by bank transfer every Friday.

"I never win." — 142 codes dropped today. It's fastest fingers, not a draw, so being early beats being lucky.

"Only for Nigeria?" — Anyone anywhere can watch and claim. Airtime PINs are Nigerian networks; cash isn't.

Running the first-100 offer without getting burned

A few things worth deciding before the first flier goes out, because each of them is expensive to change once brands are signed:

  • Cap the free tier at Basic. If the 30 days includes Premium, your first hundred brands take double the rotation and there's no room left for paying ones.
  • Put a live counter on the landing page. "63 of 100 slots left" is the entire reason this offer converts. Wire it to the real number, not a fake one.
  • Take card details at signup, charge nothing. Day-31 conversion collapses if the brand has to go and find their card again.
  • Ration prize budget against ad supply. A hundred free brands means a hundred ads and no revenue funding the freebies for a month — decide that number now.
  • Standards still apply on day one. Free ads go live unreviewed like every other ad, so the live monitor needs to be staffed from launch, not from month two.
