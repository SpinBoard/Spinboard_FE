Mobile rules the build must follow

  • Breakpoints are 380 / 768 / 1080. Design the 380 case first; the desktop mockup is the 1080 case of the same components, not a separate design.
  • Board aspect changes, board content doesn't. 16:11 on phones, 16:9 above 768px. Vertical ticker strips are hidden below 768px — never stacked or shrunk.
  • The apply field is always reachable. Below 768px it sits directly under the player and sticks to the bottom of the viewport once the player scrolls past.
  • Wins are bottom sheets. Never a centred modal, never a new page. The secret code must be copyable with one tap.
  • Tap targets are 44px minimum, and the share row on the promote screen wraps rather than scrolls horizontally.
  • Tables become cards below 768px. Every table in the desktop mockup has a card equivalent shown here; don't ship a horizontally scrolling table.
  • Bottom tab bar, no hamburger, on all three routes. Admin gets five tabs because a take-down can't be two menus deep.
